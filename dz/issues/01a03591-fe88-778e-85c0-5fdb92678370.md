Steps 8-10 of the lock design describe what dz edit should do when the issue changed underneath the editor: tell the user, offer to reapply, and re-check after they confirm. Task 6 of the lock plan was gated and never started, so edit currently fails with CONCURRENT_MODIFICATION and leaves the scratch copy on disk, naming its path. That is safe but makes the user redo the merge by hand. Needs a decision on prompting from a CLI that may not have a tty.

    Shipped. On a terminal, a conflict asks R)eload, F)orce or A)bort; the lock is released across the question. Reload reopens the editor on the newer version, force re-verifies under the lock and asks again if the file moved on, and anything ambiguous — end of input, no tty, --json — aborts. --on-conflict abort|force covers scripts and agents. Whatever a choice discards is written to a file and its path printed. The deferral said this needed a readline dependency and a pty the harness could not provide; both were wrong. A synchronous line read on fd 0 is ~30 lines, and 'script -qec' gives a real pty, so all three answers are covered end to end.
