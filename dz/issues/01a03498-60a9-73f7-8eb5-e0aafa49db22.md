Part of the single project lock design, filed separately because it is a new command with its own surface rather than a detail of the locking itself.

  dz unlock [--force]

Rules, from the design:
- Lock belongs to this hostname and its PID is gone: remove it.
- That PID is alive: refuse.
- Hostname differs, or the metadata is malformed: require --force.
- Re-read and verify the token immediately before removal, so a lock acquired between the check and the delete is not destroyed.

PID liveness is a heuristic. A recycled PID makes an abandoned lock look live and the command refuses until --force, which is the safe direction to fail in.

Shares its is-abandoned predicate with dz doctor, which reports abandoned and malformed locks but not active ones, since an active lock is transient rather than a defect.

Depends on the lock existing, so it lands with or after 01a031fe-0172.
