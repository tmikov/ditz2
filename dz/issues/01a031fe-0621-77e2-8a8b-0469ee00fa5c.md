Adding a component means hand-editing dz/config.yaml. Documented, and deliberate for v1 to keep the concept count down, but it is the largest everyday gap: --component rejects every value on a fresh project until you open an editor.

A dz component add|rm|list would close it. Worth pairing with the doctor check for issues naming a removed component, since rm is what creates those.
